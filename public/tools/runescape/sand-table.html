Sand Table

boss & raid rehearsals: the briefing, the kit, and the fight rebuilt tile by tile

Pick a fight. Each one has a briefing (requirements ticked against your hiscores, what to expect, how to get there), a kit (what to wear and the 28 slots, with a why on the odd items), and a sand table: the arena rebuilt tile by tile with a tick clock you can scrub, the boss's attacks and floor mechanics played out with the prayer and the step that answers each — then flip it to drill and it grades you.
